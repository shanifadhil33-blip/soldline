## Problem (seen live at b260c83)
In the live demo, the Harbor run sits at Waiting for approval, but its provision approval is display only: src/server/demo/seed.ts (seedWaitingItems) inserts it straight into approvals with sample refs (`sample_folder`, `sample_<deal>`) and action create_folder_and_tasks, which the executor does not run. So when Imran presses Approve in the live demo, nothing real can happen. The e2e fixture (e2e/fixtures/core.ts) works around this by deleting the seeded approvals and creating real ones with provisionPayload and services.approvals.createApproval. The live demo has no such workaround.
